## 1. Platform mechanics

| Item | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Market type | **Continuous peer-to-peer limit order book**, not an AMM. "A trade fills only when a counterparty exists." Each market is one binary YES-quoted "exchange"; books carry `bids[]`/`asks[]` of `{price, quantity}`. | [ypeng12 strategy doc](https://github.com/ypeng12/Quant.ai/blob/main/docs/PREDICTIONS_CUP_STRATEGY.md), [samyakbaid sig_client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/main/sig_client.py), [IamYanis API audit](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/API_SETTLEMENT_AUDIT.md) |
| Price range / tick | Prices are probabilities on **0–1, tick 0.005, legal range 0.005–0.995** ("integer ticks 1 through 199"); off-tick values are rejected. | [benjamingao93 orders.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot/main/src/sigbot/api/orders.py), [DanielCWeng BUILD_009](https://github.com/DanielCWeng/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/implementation/BUILD_009_LOW_LATENCY_STRATEGY_EXECUTION_CORE.md), [samyakbaid MATH.md](https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/blob/main/MATH.md) |
| Payout | Winning share pays **1 SUSQie**, losing pays 0. Cancelled/N/A outcomes refund "refundable cost". Admin can force-settle with a documented reason; runoffs wait for official results. Rules: "Each Market has two sides: YES and NO. A YES position wins if the Market resolves YES." | [Rules snippet](https://predictionscup.com/rules/), [IamYanis RI review](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/RHODE_ISLAND_SENATE_REVIEW.md) |
| Fees | **Reported zero** by two independent bots ("The contest has no fees"; "Fees: none"). One audit says "fees have not been verified", and the transactions endpoint has a `fee` type. Treat as *very likely zero, confirm in Guide*. | [nullif1ed README](https://github.com/nullif1ed/sigprediction), [grissou README](https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker), [IamYanis ACCOUNT_TEST](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/ACCOUNT_TEST.md) |
| Selling NO / shorting | Positions are signed (+YES / −NO). A sell of something you don't hold is "unbacked" and is **filled as the opposite-side buy**: sell YES @p ≡ buy NO @ 1−p. Order body uses `side: yes|no` + `action: buy|sell`. | [nullif1ed README](https://github.com/nullif1ed/sigprediction), [samyakbaid bot.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/main/bot.py) |
| Margin / collateral | **Resting orders do not reserve cash**; cash moves on fills. Platform grants **"relationship collateral"**: cash needed for linked positions (e.g., NO+NO across a race) is reduced by the guaranteed minimum payout; orders accept an optional `relationshipConstraint`. "400 insufficient funds" rejects buys beyond cash. | [IamYanis LIVE_PILOT](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/LIVE_PILOT.md), [MATH.md](https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/blob/main/MATH.md), [ZVogel1 client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZVogel1/SIG-PM-Challenge-F2026/main/src/pmcup/client.py) |
| Order types | **Limit orders only** (no `type` field). Optional `expirationDate` (clients use 30 s–15 min TTLs; expiries emit no event). **Resting orders allowed.** Atomic placement of up to **10 legs** (`POST /orders/multi-leg`; placement atomic, fills not) and batches of up to **50** (`POST /orders/batch`). One README claims IOC orders exist — **UNVERIFIED** (another bot found no IOC flag). | [orders.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot/main/src/sigbot/api/orders.py), [BUILD_009](https://github.com/DanielCWeng/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/implementation/BUILD_009_LOW_LATENCY_STRATEGY_EXECUTION_CORE.md), [grissou README](https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker) |
| Position / per-user caps | Rules say API use is "subject to … rate limits, **position limits**, technical controls" — **numbers not found**. Observed single orders of 17,751 shares and a 45,662-share sell (14 fills), so caps are not tight. | [Rules snippet](https://predictionscup.com/rules/), [Lnvqntx snapshot](https://github.com/Lnvqntx/Susquehanna-Predictions-Cup/blob/main/research/portfolio_snapshot_2026-10-09.md), [LIVE_PILOT](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/LIVE_PILOT.md) |
| Marks / leaderboard valuation | Leaderboard = cash + positions at the exchange's **"marks", based on recent trades rather than the book** (third-party); one analysis guesses "mid where two-sided, else previous mark". Leaderboard rows expose `finalTotalValue`/`pnl`, `tradesCount`. Interim standings are provisional. | [grissou README](https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker), [valuation.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/main/analysis/valuation.py), [sig_client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/main/sig_client.py) |
| Liquidity provision incentives | None documented. API docs define a **"DMM" account tier** (600 reads/200 writes per min vs 100/30 normal), implying designated market makers exist. A maker posting **20-share levels in ~230 of 237 markets around the mid** was observed; another bot says "SIG's market maker quotes roughly an 8-point spread". Whether this is SIG/house or a student is **inferred, not confirmed**. | [API audit](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/API_SETTLEMENT_AUDIT.md), [grissou LIT_REVIEW](https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/LIT_REVIEW.md), [MATH.md](https://github.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/blob/main/MATH.md) |
| Built-in arb detection | Market detail exposes `overround` and `hasArbitrageOpportunity`; `GET /relationships/constraints?violationsOnly=true` returns violations with `suggestedCorrectiveTrades`. Relationships are typed `mutually_exclusive` with `isExhaustive`; `GET /markets/{id}/nodes` gives the resolution tree. One Oct-7 audit found the tournament-scoped relationship graph **empty**. | [sig_client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/main/sig_client.py), [API audit](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/API_SETTLEMENT_AUDIT.md) |
| "Super Signal" | Platform feature aggregating positions of top traders; refreshes nightly (not real-time copy-trading). | [ypeng12 doc](https://github.com/ypeng12/Quant.ai/blob/main/docs/PREDICTIONS_CUP_STRATEGY.md) |

## 2. Rules, API, automation, dates, scoring

**Official rule text (from search snippets of [predictionscup.com/rules](https://predictionscup.com/rules/)):**
- **Bots/API explicitly allowed:** participants "may access the Platform through an application programming interface ('API') and may connect an unlimited number of automated software programs ('Bots') to their Account, provided that each Participant maintains only one (1) Account." Via the API you can "pull market data, place and cancel simulated orders, track positions, and build automated strategies," subject to "authentication requirements, rate limits, position limits, technical controls, these Official Rules, and the Platform's Terms of Service" and the posted API Reference. Sponsor may "suspend, throttle, or disqualify any participant whose Bot or API use violates these Official Rules" and may remove any bot. Bot activity counts as the participant's own.
- **One account per person.** Multiple emails/accounts/identities to get more than the initial allotment → disqualification and possible ban from future competitions. No entering via someone else's account. Current/former SIG interns and co-ops are explicitly allowed.
- **No trading on material non-public information** (third-party paraphrase: [ZVogel1 README](https://github.com/ZVogel1/SIG-PM-Challenge-F2026), [ypeng12](https://github.com/ypeng12/Quant.ai/blob/main/docs/PREDICTIONS_CUP_STRATEGY.md)).
- **Dates:** Oct 1, 2026 12:00 pm ET → **Nov 4, 2026 12:00 pm ET** ("all trades must be received prior to 12:00pm EST on November 4, 2026"). Party-winner markets carry a scheduled `settlementDate` of 2026-11-04T17:00Z (= noon EST after DST ends Nov 1). Chamber-control markets are "Freeform", settled manually once news organizations call control; runoffs wait for official results. **Sponsor may update rules, outcomes or resolution of a Market at any time, including after the resolution date.** Final standings publish after all markets resolve. ([Rules](https://predictionscup.com/rules/), [Series page](https://sig.thesuper.market/docs/tournaments-and-organizations), [RI review](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/RHODE_ISLAND_SENATE_REVIEW.md), [LIT_REVIEW](https://github.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/blob/main/analysis/reports/LIT_REVIEW.md))
- **Scoring:** pure final **verified total SUSQies balance** after all markets resolve (no Sharpe, no risk adjustment). Must make ≥1 trade to be ranked. Ties split prizes (1st, 1st, 3rd). Real-time "Leaderboards" tab, provisional. Prizes **$30,000 / $5,000 / $2,500 USD**, where permitted by law (the $2,500 on the homepage is 3rd place, not a conflict). Everyone starts with **100,000 SUSQies** (non-monetary). ([Rules](https://predictionscup.com/rules/), [press release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260922468550/en/Susquehanna-Predictions-Launches-Predictions-Cup-for-University-Students))
- **Eligibility:** actively enrolled undergrad/grad/PhD (or local equivalent) at an accredited institution, 18+/legal adult, able to read English, resident of an "Eligible Jurisdiction" (list includes US, UK, Ireland, Australia with regional carve-outs for parts of Australia and India; a syndicated press release lists China but the rules reportedly omit it — **UNVERIFIED**). Free to enter. ([Rules](https://predictionscup.com/rules/), [financialcontent copy](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-9-23-susquehanna-predictions-launches-predictions-cup-for-university-students))

**API (documented `v1`, Scalar/OpenAPI at [sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/docs](https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1/docs); reconstructed from clients):**
- Base `https://sig.thesuper.market/api/v1` (some clients default to `https://www.thesuper.market/api/v1`). Auth `Authorization: Bearer <API key>`; keys created under **My Profile → API Keys** with `read` and `trade` scopes. Must confirm email, **register for the tournament, and accept current Terms** or every call 403s. "Pending enrolment: make one trade to join." ([benjamingao93 README](https://github.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot), [samyakbaid sig_client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samyakbaid/Susquehanna-Super-Market/main/sig_client.py))
- Reads: `GET /account`, `/tournaments`, `/tournaments/{slug}` (fields `id`, `slug`, `myBalance`, `startDate`, `endDate`, **`memberCount`**, `marketCount`), `/tournaments/{slug}/markets`, `/markets` (filters `tournamentId,status,category,search,sort,ids,is_multi_outcome`), `/markets/{id}`, `/markets/{id}/nodes`, `/exchanges`, `/exchanges/{id}/price`, `/exchanges/prices?ids=` (≤100 ids → `bestBid,bestAsk,latestPrice,spread`; all 237 in 3 reads), `/exchanges/{id}/orderbook?depth=` (≤200), `/exchanges/{id}/price-history?resolution=1h`, `/exchanges/{id}/trades`, `/relationships`, `/relationships/constraints`, `/orders`, `/orders/{id}`, `/orders/{id}/fills`, `/portfolio/fills`, `/portfolio/positions`, `/tournaments/{slug}/portfolio/{positions|pnl|transactions}`, `/tournaments/{slug}/leaderboard?period=all`. Cursor pagination (`pagination.nextCursor/hasMore`). ([DanielCWeng client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DanielCWeng/predictions-cup/main/src/predictions_cup/sig/client.py), [ZVogel1 client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZVogel1/SIG-PM-Challenge-F2026/main/src/pmcup/client.py))
- Writes: `POST /orders` `{exchangeId, side, action, quantity(int), price, tournamentId, idempotencyKey, expirationDate?}` → `{orderId, quantityTraded, totalCost, fillPrice, all{effectiveEntryCost…}}`; `POST /orders/multi-leg` (≤10 legs), `POST /orders/batch` (≤50), `DELETE /orders/{id}`, `POST /orders/cancel-all` (207/422 partial results). **Every order must carry `tournamentId` or it trades the public (non-tournament) book.** ([orders.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot/main/src/sigbot/api/orders.py), [BUILD_009](https://github.com/DanielCWeng/predictions-cup/blob/main/docs/implementation/BUILD_009_LOW_LATENCY_STRATEGY_EXECUTION_CORE.md))
- **Rate limits (documented): 100 reads/min + 30 writes/min per account, shared across all keys** (DMM tier 600/200). 429 carries `Retry-After: 60`. Error codes: `429 RATE_LIMITED`, `409 REQUEST_IN_FLIGHT` (same idempotency key holds a 90 s lease), `503 TX_CONFLICT`/`SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, `502 ORDER_STATUS_UNKNOWN` (retry with same key), `VALIDATION_ERROR`. ([benjamingao93 client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot/main/src/sigbot/api/client.py), [API audit](https://github.com/IamYanis/Susquehanna-Prediction-Cup-Strategy/blob/main/API_SETTLEMENT_AUDIT.md))
- **Realtime:** `POST /realtime/token` → Supabase Realtime private broadcast (`supabaseUrl`, `anonKey`, 3-h token). Channels `tournament:{tid}:market:{mid}` and `user:{profileId}`; events `market_batch` (books + trades, `delivery.revision`, `marketSettled`, `resyncRequired`, `nextExpiryAt`), `book_dirty`, `market_settled`, `account_batch` (fills). **~100-channel cap per connection** ("ChannelRateLimitReached"), ~250 ms batching, pushes don't count against REST limits; batches are sent once (dropped updates possible on quiet markets → periodic REST reconciliation). ([realtime.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjamingao93/SIG-prediction-bot/main/src/sigbot/api/realtime.py))
- **Observed engine behavior:** at the Oct 1 open, writes took 14–36 s (but landed); reads 0.5–1.5 s; list endpoint first to 503 under load; a "2 Oct 11:31 incident" of 429s; portfolio endpoint 16 s+ under load; book data may be cached ≤1 s. ([grissou scenario.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/grissou/prediction-market-maker/main/tests/scenario.py), [nl2992 sig_client.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nl2992/sig-arb/main/sig_client.py))
- **Undocumented web-app endpoints** (cookie/Supabase-session auth: `/api/markets/page-data`, `/api/markets/{id}/orders`, `/api/trading/orders/{quote|place|cancel}`, `/api/markets/{id}/news`) are used by one repo; this is scraping the UI, not the published API, and is subject to bot protection — risky under the "API Reference / ToS" clause. ([nl2992 README](https://github.com/nl2992/sig-arb))
